# Sentences for the 💬 card: each uses the word and teaches something beyond
# it — the concepts gifted programs test (categories, parts and functions,
# opposites and comparison, families, where things go, numbers, cause and
# effect, words with two meanings). Every fact must be true and unarguable
# (see the Gifted fairness rules). Short: mostly words he can already read.
#
# word | concept | sentence — {braces} mark the word (or its form) to light up.
# Any word with sentences gets the 💬 button, so new vocabulary levels just add lines.
# build.py speaks them (sentences/ audio + word timings in sentences.json).
